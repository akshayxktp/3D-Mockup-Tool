# Modifications

This is a modified copy of [Motion Studio](https://github.com/appariciojunior/motion-studio-open).

Apache-2.0 section 4(b) requires a derivative work to carry prominent notices
stating that files were changed. The upstream `NOTICE` restates the same
obligation. The list below is that statement; the upstream `LICENSE` and
`NOTICE` are unchanged and travel with this copy.

All changes are additive and confined to the Mockup section. No upstream
feature was removed.

## Changed files

|---|---|
| `three3d/mockupControls.ts` | New controls: split Body/Glass Exposure, Light Softness, a Blur group (Blur, Focus Size, Falloff, Bokeh, Click to Focus, Focus X/Y) and a Guides group (3×3 / 6×6 grid). |

## Known limitations

Two things are understood and deliberately left alone, recorded so they are not
rediscovered as bugs:

**Nothing Phone (3) has no black finish.** The mesh carries one back texture,
the white variant's, and the plate tones, the silkscreen and the red accent
square all live in that image. The Finish control works by re-hueing material
colour, which multiplies the map, so a black finish drives the whole texture to
black and takes the accent and the plate edges with it. The real black Phone (3)
ships a different back texture rather than a tint of the white one. Only White
is offered; `ENCLOSURE_BY_MATERIAL` in `three3d/mockup.ts` already resolves this
device's body correctly and is what a real black variant would build on.

**Nothing Phone (4a) Pro shows the Android navigation pill.** Its whole front is
one textured plate, and that texture is a photograph of a running phone — so the
gesture pill near the bottom of the display is painted into it, along with the
bezel and the punch-hole camera. `scripts/_inset_screen_glb.cjs` lays the Screen
quad over the active display area, which is where the pill also sits, so
uploaded artwork covers it in the middle of the panel but the plate's own pixels
remain wherever the artwork does not reach. The punch-hole is inside the display
area for the same reason.
